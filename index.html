Calisto

Calisto is a Bengal Queen of 4 years old with a personality of your typical curious infant and an energy of a husky. Her name came from greek myths: a nymph. She is very playful, communicable and will welcome anyone who tries to be nice to her. When she is not beating up Dionis or watching birds she likes to sleep with her head upside down with her paws covering her eyes. She is to be as a queen, and treated nicely. Or else buy new shoes.

Dionis

Dionis is the smartest cat alive. From humble street begginings he was picked up by us when he was just a few months old. He immediately knew how to use the litterbox, was polite and was nurtured back to health after a short while. Now he lives his days in peace and a lot of food, constantly caring for his sister Calisto. His favorite food is shrimp and his favorite activity is terrorizing the neighbor cat.
