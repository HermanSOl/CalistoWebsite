Calisto

Calisto is a Bengal Queen of 4 years old with a personality of your typical curious infant and an energy of a husky. Her name came from greek myths: a nymph. She is very playful, communicable and will welcome anyone who tries to be nice to her. When she is not beating up Dionis or watching birds she likes to sleep with her head upside down with her paws covering her eyes. She is to be treated as a queen, and treated nicely. Or else buy new shoes.
